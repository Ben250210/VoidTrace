## What VoidTrace does, and does not do

VoidTrace only **reads**:

- Warframe's log file (`EE.log`) and the debug output Warframe already broadcasts
- what is on your screen (screenshots of the relic reward screen and riven cards)
- public data from Warframe.Market, and, only if you sign in, your own orders and auctions

It **never** changes game files, reads or writes the game's memory, injects code into the game, or
sends key presses or mouse clicks to the game. Prices are shown in its own windows; whispers are copied
to your clipboard for you to paste yourself.

change at any time. Being read-only does not guarantee that using it is permitted or safe.
